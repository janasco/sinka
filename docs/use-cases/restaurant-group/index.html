Restaurants: every manager sees the delivery change

Supplier invoices, delivery changes and health-inspector mail copied to every location manager. Sinka fans one operations inbox out across the group.

Photo by binmassam on Pixabay

The setup

Operations director Wren fans orders@ out to three location managers plus herself. Each manager runs their kitchen from their own inbox.

How the mail flows

  1. A fish supplier emails a substitution at 6:05am.
  2. All three managers hold the identical notice before service prep.
  3. Menus adjust in time; the invoice thread stays with the manager who signed it.
  4. orders@ keeps the paper trail for supplier disputes and audits.

Why Sinka fits here

  • Substitutions stop being surprises at 11am.
  • Managers keep their own supplier threads instead of one tangled login.
  • New managers get the full flow on day one, cut off cleanly on exit.

Watch out for

  • Delivery drivers will reply to whoever answered — insist invoices CC orders@ so finance sees them.
  • Health-inspector correspondence deserves its own address, not a shared food inbox.
Try this setup yourself. Sinka is free and open source — one inbox in, every teammate covered.
