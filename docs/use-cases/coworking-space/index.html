Coworking: the tour booking reaches community, not a queue

Member requests, tour bookings and parcel notices copied to community and ops teams. Sinka fans one hello inbox out across a coworking space.

The setup

Space manager Hank fans hello@ out to two community hosts and the ops lead. Everyone reads the same member mail.

How the mail flows

  1. A prospect asks for a same-day tour at 10am.
  2. Hosts and ops both hold the identical request within minutes.
  3. The free host meets them at noon instead of losing them.
  4. hello@ keeps the request record that explains occupancy dips.

Why Sinka fits here

  • Tours stop depending on who checks the login.
  • Maintenance requests reach ops with the member's words attached.
  • New hosts get the full flow on day one.

Watch out for

  • Parcels will bury humans — keep deliveries on a separate address or filter.
  • Access and billing issues need tighter handling than tour requests; separate the addresses.
Try this setup yourself. Sinka is free and open source — one inbox in, every teammate covered.
