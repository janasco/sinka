Warehouse: the floor sees the delivery change

ASN notices, shortage claims and delivery slots copied to office and floor. Sinka fans one warehouse inbox out to desks and forklifts alike.

The setup

Warehouse manager Olga fans goods@ out to the office, two supervisors and a floor tablet address. Everyone reads the same notices.

How the mail flows

  1. A supplier moves Friday's delivery from dock 3 to dock 7.
  2. Office and floor both hold the identical notice before the shift.
  3. Staff and staging go to the right dock first time.
  4. goods@ keeps the ASN and shortage-claim trail for chargebacks.

Why Sinka fits here

  • Dock changes stop dying on one office screen.
  • Shortage photos arrive where the pallets are.
  • Shift supervisors cover each other without sharing logins.

Watch out for

  • Tablet inboxes need the same retention policy as desks, or the floor becomes an accidental archive.
  • Reply discipline matters: confirmations go back to the supplier, not just around the team.
Try this setup yourself. Sinka is free and open source — one inbox in, every teammate covered.
