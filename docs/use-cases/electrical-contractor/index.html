Electrical: estimates and call-outs to the right hands

Call-outs and certificate requests copied to estimators and electricians. Sinka gives an electrical contractor whole-team coverage from one address.

Photo by jarmoluk on Pixabay

The setup

Operations lead Tess fans info@ out to two estimators and six electricians. Office staff quote from desks; sparkies read from site cabins.

How the mail flows

  1. A client sends updated plans at 4pm for a Friday quote.
  2. Estimators and the assigned sparkie all hold the identical plans within minutes.
  3. The quote goes out Thursday from the estimator's own address.
  4. info@ keeps the version history of every plan revision received.

Why Sinka fits here

  • Plan PDFs arrive byte-identical — no re-upload chain.
  • Apprentices see real job mail without the office password.
  • When a sparkie leaves, one revoked password, zero disruption.

Watch out for

  • Name plan revisions by date in your replies; the inbox copies arrive in order but humans still need the discipline.
  • Compliance certificates deserve a dedicated address so they never hide among quotes.
