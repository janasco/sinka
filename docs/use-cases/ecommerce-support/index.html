E-commerce: support and warehouse read the same order

Order queries, returns and address changes copied to support and warehouse. Sinka stops the 'support said yes, warehouse never heard' gap.

Photo by Preis_King on Pixabay

The setup

Founder Pablo fans help@ out to two support agents and the warehouse lead. Everyone sees the same customer mail.

How the mail flows

  1. A customer changes their delivery address at 10am.
  2. Support and warehouse both hold the identical change within minutes.
  3. The parcel diverts before the 2pm courier pickup.
  4. help@ keeps the promise record: what was agreed, and when.

Why Sinka fits here

  • Address changes stop falling between two systems.
  • Return approvals reach the bench that receives the box.
  • Peak-season temps get real copies, revoked in January.

Watch out for

  • Cut-off times rule: changes after courier pickup need a recall process, not just an email.
  • Keep payment disputes on a separate address with tighter access.
