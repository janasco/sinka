Photography studio: the date gets claimed, not queued

Shoot enquiries copied to every photographer's own inbox. Sinka lets a studio answer fast without sharing a login — first available shooter wins the date.

Photo by u_ojq9rdzqq7 on Pixabay

The setup

Studio owner Finn fans hello@ out to three photographers. Each replies with their own portfolio link and their own availability.

How the mail flows

  1. A couple asks about a September Saturday with venue and package details.
  2. All three photographers hold the identical enquiry within minutes.
  3. The free one replies with portfolio and a holds-the-date offer the same day.
  4. hello@ keeps the enquiry log that settles 'who asked first' disputes.

Why Sinka fits here

  • Replies carry a photographer's voice and portfolio, not a generic alias.
  • Second shooters see the brief without a forwarding chain.
  • Associate shooters get copies per wedding, revoked after delivery.

Watch out for

  • Hold dates in one shared calendar the moment you reply, or two photographers promise one Saturday.
  • Gallery-delivery links are big; each inbox needs the quota for them.
Try this setup yourself. Sinka is free and open source — one inbox in, every teammate covered.
