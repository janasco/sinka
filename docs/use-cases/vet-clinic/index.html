Vet clinic: the whole team sees the sick Labrador

Appointment requests, lab results and worried owners, copied to the whole vet team. Sinka fans one clinic inbox out to vets, nurses and reception alike.

The setup

Head vet Marco fans care@ out to two vets, two nurses and reception. Night emergencies go to whoever is on call, from their own inbox.

How the mail flows

  1. A lab emails blood results at 11pm.
  2. The on-call vet holds the identical result within minutes, on their own phone.
  3. Morning reception sees the same thread and books the follow-up without asking.
  4. care@ keeps the complete clinical correspondence in arrival order.

Why Sinka fits here

  • Nurses triage from their own inboxes without the vet's password.
  • Photo and video attachments of symptoms arrive intact everywhere.
  • On-call rotation is just whose inbox gets attention — no reconfiguration.

Watch out for

  • Lab-result threads must still be filed in the patient record; the inbox copy is the alert, not the archive.
  • Owner anxiety peaks at night — a fast human reply from the on-call vet is the product here.
Try this setup yourself. Sinka is free and open source — one inbox in, every teammate covered.
