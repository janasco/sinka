HR: every recruiter sees every application

Job applications copied to every recruiter's own inbox. Sinka fans one careers address out across HR — no shared login, no lost candidates.

Photo by AymaneJed on Pixabay

The setup

HR lead Zara fans careers@ out to four recruiters plus the coordinator. Each recruiter owns their departments from their own inbox.

How the mail flows

  1. Thirty applications land for a backend role on Monday.
  2. All four recruiters hold the identical batch within minutes.
  3. The engineering recruiter shortlists Tuesday instead of next week.
  4. careers@ keeps the complete applicant record for compliance.

Why Sinka fits here

  • Hiring managers can be added per role without new systems.
  • Recruiters keep their own pipelines and sent history.
  • Rejection mail comes from a person, which candidates prefer.

Watch out for

  • One role, one owner — or candidates get two different rejections.
  • Application data is regulated; retention and deletion rules must cover every copy.
Try this setup yourself. Sinka is free and open source — one inbox in, every teammate covered.
