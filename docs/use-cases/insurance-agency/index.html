Insurance: the claim reaches a broker, not a queue

Claims and renewal queries copied to every broker's inbox. Sinka gives an insurance agency shared awareness without a shared login.

Photo by 089photoshootings on Pixabay

The setup

Agency principal Ingrid fans claims@ out to five brokers. Each owns their book and replies from their own address.

How the mail flows

  1. A storm-damage claim arrives Saturday with photos.
  2. All five brokers hold the identical claim within minutes.
  3. The owning broker starts the claim Monday with photos already reviewed.
  4. claims@ keeps the timestamped record insurers and auditors want.

Why Sinka fits here

  • Storm surges copy to everyone — no single point of failure.
  • Claim photos arrive full-resolution in every copy.
  • Producer changes are one revoked password, not a mailbox migration.

Watch out for

  • Ownership must be obvious (policy number in subject) or two brokers open two claims.
  • Regulated content multiplies with copies — keep the same compliance rules on all five inboxes.
Try this setup yourself. Sinka is free and open source — one inbox in, every teammate covered.
