Music school: the enquiry reaches the teacher

Trial-lesson enquiries copied to teachers and admin. Sinka fans one admissions inbox out across a music school.

Photo by dengyuanhao2014 on Pixabay

The setup

Administrator Gina fans learn@ out to herself and twelve teachers. Each teacher replies for their own instrument from their own inbox.

How the mail flows

  1. A parent asks about beginner violin Saturday.
  2. Violin teacher and admin both hold the identical enquiry within minutes.
  3. A Tuesday trial is offered Sunday instead of next week.
  4. learn@ keeps the enrolment record: instrument, teacher, start date.

Why Sinka fits here

  • Twelve teachers, zero forwarding — enquiries route themselves by instrument.
  • Teachers reply with their voice and their availability.
  • New teachers join the flow with one address added.

Watch out for

  • Timetables live in one place — the inbox informs, the timetable decides.
  • Child safeguarding details need tighter handling than trial enquiries; separate the addresses.
Try this setup yourself. Sinka is free and open source — one inbox in, every teammate covered.
