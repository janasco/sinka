Admissions: every counsellor sees every applicant

Admissions enquiries copied to every counsellor's own inbox. Sinka fans one admissions address out to the team through application season — no shared login, no missed applicants.

Photo by sasint on Pixabay

The setup

Head of admissions Noor fans admissions@ out to six regional counsellors, each working their territory from their own inbox and replying in their own name.

How the mail flows

  1. An applicant asks about a missing transcript on a Sunday.
  2. All six counsellors hold the identical query within minutes.
  3. The right regional counsellor replies Monday with the applicant's file already open.
  4. admissions@ keeps the complete, timestamped record for audit and appeals.

Why Sinka fits here

  • Seasonal staff get real copies on day one, cut off cleanly in February.
  • Each counsellor's sent folder becomes their own case history.
  • No CRM needed for teams that live in Gmail.

Watch out for

  • Split territories clearly (region in subject conventions) so two counsellors don't both chase one applicant.
  • Deadline-week volume copies fine, but remind counsellors their inbox quota must fit attachments like portfolios.
Try this setup yourself. Sinka is free and open source — one inbox in, every teammate covered.
